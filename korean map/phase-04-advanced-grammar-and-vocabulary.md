# PHASE 4 — ADVANCED GRAMMAR AND VOCABULARY

**Korean Roadmap · Levels 10–12 of 18**

> **Phase goal.** Bridge the gap between upper-intermediate communication and advanced stylistic fluency. # LEVEL 10 — ADVANCED GRAMMAR PATTERNS

> **Goal.** Master the TOPIK Level 4–5 grammar architecture: multi-clause hypothetical conditions, nuanced supposition, regret, inevitability, and concessive constructions. Transition from "correct sentences" to expressing exact degrees of probability, intention, and emotional stance.

## 10.1 — Supposition, Probability, and Deductive Stance

### Module 10.1.1 — Projected Background: -(으)ㄹ 텐데
- [ ] Understand `-(으)ㄹ 텐데` as combining future expectation/supposition `-(으)ㄹ 터이다` with background connector `-ㄴ/은/는데`.
- [ ] Contrast `비가 올 텐데 우산을 가져가세요` (strong anticipation offering advice) with `비가 올 텐데요` (sentence ending expressing concern or presumption).
- [ ] Drill the past supposition variant: `-았/었을 텐데` (e.g. `많이 피곤했을 텐데 푹 쉬세요`).

### Module 10.1.2 — Observable Evidence: -(으)ㄴ/는 모양이다
- [ ] Understand `-(으)ㄴ/는 모양이다` as deduction based on observable external signs or evidence.
- [ ] Contrast with `-나 보다` (informal, intuitive deduction) vs `-(으)ㄴ/는 모양이다` (written/spoken, based on visible cues).
- [ ] Conjugate correctly across tenses: `-은 모양이다` (past), `-는 모양이다` (present verb), `-(으)ㄴ 모양이다` (adjective), `-(으)ㄹ 모양이다` (imminent future).

> **Module completion gate (10.1).** Record 6 spontaneous sentences explaining real situations using `-(으)ㄹ 텐데` and `-(으)ㄴ/는 모양이다` without looking at notes.

---

## 10.2 — Equivalence and Regret

### Module 10.2.1 — Virtual Equivalence: -(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다 and -(으)ㄴ 셈 치다
- [ ] Master `-(으)ㄴ/는 셈이다` ("it amounts to", "practically considered as"): `거의 다 끝난 셈이다` (it's practically finished).
- [ ] Master `-(으)ㄴ 셈 치다` ("let's consider it as...", "pretend that"): `속는 셈 치고 한번 믿어 보세요` (give it the benefit of the doubt).

### Module 10.2.2 — Narrow Escapes and Remorse: -(으)ㄹ 뻔했다 and -(으)ㄹ 걸 그랬다
- [ ] Master `-(으)ㄹ 뻔했다` for events that almost happened (usually accompanied by `하마터면`, `거의`): `늦잠을 자서 비행기를 놓칠 뻔했다`.
- [ ] Master `-(으)ㄹ 걸 그랬다` for expressing regret about an unchosen path: `좀 더 일찍 출발할 걸 그랬어요`.

> **Module completion gate (10.2).** Create a 2-minute oral monologue describing an emergency or close call using both `-(으)ㄹ 뻔했다` and `-(으)ㄴ 셈이다`.

---

## 10.3 — Inevitability and Natural Tendency

### Module 10.3.1 — Inevitable Consequence: -기 마련이다 and -법이다
- [ ] Use `-기 마련이다` for natural laws and inevitable outcomes: `시간이 지나면 상처는 아물기 마련이다`.
- [ ] Contrast with `-(으)ㄴ/는 법이다` ("it is the way of things"): `사람은 누구나 실수하기 마련이다 / 마련인 법이다`.

### Module 10.3.2 — Limit and Monopolisation: -기 십상이다 and -기 일쑤이다
- [ ] Use `-기 십상이다` for high probabilities of negative outcomes: `급하게 서두르면 실수하기 십상이다`.
- [ ] Use `-기 일쑤이다` for chronic undesirable habitual actions: `그는 약속 시간을 어기기 일쑤였다`.

> **Module completion gate (10.3).** State 4 universal principles of human behaviour using `-기 마련이다` and `-는 법이다`.

---

## 10.4 — Concession, Choice, and Preference

### Module 10.4.1 — Hypothetical Concession: -더라도 and -(으)ㄹ망정
- [ ] Master `-더라도` ("even if", "granted that"): `아무리 힘들더라도 끝까지 완수해야 합니다`.
- [ ] Master the elevated written concessive `-(으)ㄹ망정` / `-(으)ㄹ지언정` ("even though I may... I will never..."): `굶어 죽을망정 부정한 돈은 받지 않겠다`.

### Module 10.4.2 — Extreme Preference: -느니 차라리
- [ ] Master `-느니 (차라리)` ("rather than doing X, I would rather do Y"): `그런 대우를 받느니 차라리 회사를 그만두겠다`.
- [ ] Contrast with `-기보다는` (mild preference) vs `-느니 차라리` (categorical aversion).

> **Module completion gate (10.4).** Roleplay a difficult ethical decision using `-느니 차라리` and `-더라도`.

---

## 10.5 — Simultaneous Circumstance and Intentionality

### Module 10.5.1 — Opportunistic Action: -는 김에 and -는 길에
- [ ] Master `-는 김에` ("while taking the opportunity of X"): `서점에 가는 김에 제 책도 한 권 사다 주세요`.
- [ ] Master `-는 길에` ("on one's physical way to/from"): `퇴근하는 길에 마트에 들렀어요`.

### Module 10.5.2 — Pretense: -(으)ㄴ/는 척하다 (체하다)
- [ ] Master `-(으)ㄴ/는 척하다` ("pretend to"): `알면서도 모르는 척했다`, `바쁜 척하지 마세요`.
- [ ] Apply past, present, and adjective forms correctly: `아는 척하다`, `들은 척도 안 하다`.

---

## 10.6 — Degree, Extent, and Proportion

### Module 10.6.1 — Proportional Development: -(으)ㄹ수록 and -(으)면 -(으)ㄹ수록
- [ ] Master `-(으)ㄹ수록` ("the more... the more..."): `한국어는 배울수록 흥미진진하다`.
- [ ] Apply to double-clause structures: `연습을 많이 하면 할수록 실력이 늡니다`.

### Module 10.6.2 — Extreme Degree: -(으)ㄹ 정도로
- [ ] Master `-(으)ㄹ 정도로` / `-(으)ㄹ 정도이다` ("to the extent that..."): `숨이 턱에 찰 정도로 뛰었다`.
- [ ] Construct idioms of physical extent: `눈코 뜰 새 없이 바쁠 정도이다`.

---

## 10.7 — Exclusion and Contrastive Emphasis

### Module 10.7.1 — Far From It: -기는커녕 / 은/는커녕
- [ ] Master `-기는커녕` ("far from doing X, cannot even do Y"): `상금은커녕 본전도 못 건졌다`, `쉬기는커녕 밤을 새웠다`.
- [ ] Pair with negative endings: `못하다`, `없다`, `않다`.

### Module 10.7.2 — Dual Aspects: -는 한편
- [ ] Master `-(으)ㄴ/는 한편` ("on one hand... on the other hand..."): `신제품 개발에 주력하는 한편 해외 시장 개척에도 힘쓰고 있다`.

---

## 10.8 — Discourse Synthesis and Transition

### Module 10.8.1 — Causative Negative Impact: -느라고
- [ ] Master `-느라고` for subject continuity where energy in clause 1 causes an adverse clause 2: `시험공부 하느라고 잠을 못 잤어요`.
- [ ] Enforce the three structural constraints: identical subjects, action verbs only, negative/burdensome result.

### Module 10.8.2 — Hearsay and Confirmation: -다면서요?
- [ ] Master `-다면서요?` / `-라면서요?` to verify hearsay with the interlocutor: `다음 달에 한국으로 전근 가신다면서요?`.

## 11.1 — The Mechanics of Hanja in Modern Korean

### Module 11.1.1 — Morphemes, Roots, and Compound Formation
- [ ] Understand that Korean does not require writing Chinese characters by hand; it requires **auditory and semantic root recognition**.
- [ ] Understand homophonic divergence: why `전` can mean 前 (front/before), 電 (electricity), 全 (entire), or 戰 (war).
- [ ] Learn the context-clue heuristic: identifying the root family by surrounding topic domain.

### Module 11.1.2 — Affixes and Prefixes in Sino-Korean
- [ ] Master negative prefixes: 不/부/불 (부족, 불가능, 불안), 無/무 (무료, 무단, 무한), 非/비 (비공식, 비정상), 未/미 (미완성, 미성년).
- [ ] Master directional and temporal prefixes: 前/전 (전임, 전직), 後/후 (후배, 후유증), 再/재 (재발, 재시험).

## 12.4 — Korean Mimetic and Onomatopoeic Dynamics (의성어·의태어)
